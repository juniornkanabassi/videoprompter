Kylian Mbappé DOES NOT DESERVE the Ballon d’Or

Mbappé has recently come out saying he deserves the Ballon d’Or and sees himself as the best player in the World, and the main argument he’s put forward to support his case is the fact that he’s finished with the Golden Boot in 3 major competitions. 

Namely, La Liga, the Champions League, and the World Cup.

But before I argue why I think he doesn’t deserve to win it, let’s first familiarise ourselves with the Ballon d’Or criteria

The main 3 criteria on which the Ballon d’Or is awarded are as follows: 

1. Individual performances, decisive and impressive character
2. Team performances and achievements 
3. Class and fair play

With these criteria in mind, I’m sorry to say, Kylian Mbappe is nowhere near the most deserving winner. 

Let’s start off with the obvious: the fact that Mbappe did not win a single trophy this past season. And in a year where there is not much separating a lot of the candidates performance-wise, team achievements become even more influential in such a tightly contested Ballon d’Or race. And the fact that Mbappé hasn’t won a single one really hurts his case.

Next, let’s analyse Mbappé's case for the Ballon d'Or according to the first criteria and see if it holds up to scrutiny. 

While it is true that Mbappé won the Golden Boot in La Liga, the Champions League, and the World Cup, we don't award the Ballon d’Or solely to the player who was the top goal scorer in every competition. 

We have other trophies we award to the best goal scorer, and those are the Gerd Müller Trophy and European Golden Boot. Which, oh yeah, Mbappé did not win because Harry Kane still outscored him over the course of the season.

And before anybody tries to object that Harry Kane was stat padding in the Bundesliga, let's compare the two’s goal-scoring records in the competition they shared in common, the Champions League, in which Harry Kane only scored one less goal than Mbappé, but Mbappé was the one stat padding in the league phase. 

9 of Mbappé's 15 Champions League goals last season came against Olympiacos, Marseille, and Kairat Almaty. Three teams that failed to qualify for the round of 16. 

Compare that to Harry Kane last season, who scored in five consecutive Champions League knockout stage games. Including scoring home and away against Kylian Mbappe’s Real Madrid.

So if the main argument put forward to support Mbappé’s case for the Ballon d’Or is his goalscoring prowess. Then, under this logic, shouldn't Harry Kane be ahead of Mbappé, as over the totality of the season he was a better goalscorer than him?

And if you're still not convinced by my argument and you still think Mbappé deserves to win the Ballon d'Or, let me ask you this. 

Was Mbappé the best player in La Liga? No. Was he the best player in the Champions League? No. Was he the best player at the World Cup? No. 

So how can a player who wasn’t the best player in any of the three biggest competitions he participated in be the best player in the world?
