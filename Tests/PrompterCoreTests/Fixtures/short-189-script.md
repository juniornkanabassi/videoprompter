Enzo Maresca is a hypocrite.

After Man City lost to PSG, beIN Sports asked him if PSG had an advantage because they got to rest players over the weekend.

He agreed. 

He said PSG can manage their players in the games before the Champions League, but for City it's different.

Funny, because at Chelsea, Maresca did exactly the same thing.

In the 2024-25 Conference League, he left Palmer, Fofana and Lavia out of the squad entirely, then made 11 changes and still beat Panathinaikos 4-1.

His explanation?

"We make changes because we have 24, 25 players, all fit, all good enough to play different competitions."

He rested his stars in the weaker competition to keep them fresh for when things mattered most. That's exactly what PSG are doing.

And yet nobody called that unfair. It was just good squad management.

So why is it only a problem now?

Luis Enrique answered it himself. 

He called it the typical noise around a club like PSG. 

Before he arrived, all he heard was that PSG had never won the Champions League. 

And the reason people always gave was that they played in Ligue 1.

Now that PSG have won it, lots of people are complaining.

There's a name for this. Tall poppy syndrome.

Picture a field of poppies. If one grows taller than the rest, you cut it down so the field looks even again.

People resent PSG for being successful, so they look for any reason to cut them down.

For over a decade, the weak league was the stick to beat PSG with. 

Ligue 1 was too easy, so PSG would never be ready for the Champions League.

Then PSG won it, and now the same weak league is suddenly an unfair advantage.

That's moving the goalposts.

The test used to be win the Champions League. PSG passed it. So now the test is win it without resting anyone.

PSG don't have an unfair advantage. 

They just use their squad, the same way Maresca did at Chelsea.
