Why is Yamal a better Champions League player than Doué?

A while back, I made a video arguing that Doué was a better Champions League player than Lamine Yamal. 

And given how both have started this Champions League campaign. 

Let’s compare their respective Champions League résumés. 

Specifically, what they’ve done against Europe’s elite, using opponents ranked in UEFA’s Top 10 coefficient before each season began.

I’ll look across their entire Champions League careers and compare their numbers per 90 minutes to keep the comparison fair.

When doing so, we see the following.

Doué’s goal contributions rate is more than double Yamal’s.

He creates more chances and has higher expected assists.

He makes more progressive carries, has more goal-creating actions and records more interceptions.

His expected assisted goals are higher per 90 too. 

This adds up the scoring chances of every shot he sets up, whether his teammate scores or misses.

So in short, for the time he plays, his passes create more goal threat.

And he’s dispossessed less often too.

He’s also statistically a more efficient finisher. 

He’s converted a greater share of his shots and scored further above his non-penalty expected goals.

So in light of these numbers, what exactly makes Yamal the better Champions League player?
