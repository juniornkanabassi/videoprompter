Here's an interesting stat.

Vitinha has 3 times as many Champions League goals against Barça alone as Pedri has in his entire Champions League career.

And with that fact in mind, I'd like to know.

Why is Pedri better than Vitinha?

Because anytime somebody even puts Vitinha in the same conversation as Pedri, people act as though the comparison itself is ridiculous.

Let's consider what they've done in a competition they share in common, the Champions League.

Since the start of 2024/25 season, including their first 3 games this season. 

Vitinha’s average on-ball actions are deeper than Pedri’s, showing he’s the deeper of the two midfielders.

Yet in spite of this, he still produces more goal contributions per 90 than Pedri.

And before you tell me goals and assists aren't how you judge a midfielder, let's look at what he's doing before the final pass.

Before last season's Champions League final, Opta had Vitinha leading the competition for secondary chances created per 90.

Which measures the pass before the pass that sets up a shot.

He was among the leading midfielders for build-up involvement and line-breaking passes too.

And among midfielders with at least 450 minutes, nobody averaged more progressive carries per 90.

Going back to our per-90 comparison with Pedri since 2024/25, Vitinha completes more passes and switches the play more often.

He has fewer poor touches that lose possession. And he's dispossessed less often too.

On top of all of this, Vitinha has been named in UEFA's official Champions League Team of the Season for each of the last 3 seasons.

In those same 3 seasons, Pedri hasn't made it once.

So, given what he's producing from a deeper position, what exactly makes Pedri clear of Vitinha?
