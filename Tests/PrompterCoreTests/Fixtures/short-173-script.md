Barcelona will not win the Champions League because of Hansi Flick's stubbornness and insistence on playing with an offside trap.  
  
And before all you Barça fans get upset and emotional, please watch the video in its entirety before going to the comments.
  
Barça is 2nd only to PSG in cumulative goals scored over the last two Champions League campaigns. 

In fact, if you look more closely, Barcelona is the team that scores the most goals per game of any team in Europe, averaging 2.88 goals per game. Higher than Bayern’s 2.64 and PSG’s 2.44.  
  
Yet despite this, of the teams that have reached at least 1 Champions League quarter-final in these last 2 seasons, no team has conceded more goals than Barcelona’s 44. 
  
Amongst quarter-finalists, only Atletico Madrid and Real Madrid concede more expected goals per game than Barcelona. Barça concedes 1.64 expected goals per game. 

Compare that to Bayern and PSG, who respectively only concede 1.36 and 1.30 expected goals per game. And when looking at big chances conceded per game, only Atletico Madrid’s 2.88 beats Barcelona’s 2.62.  

And to prove that this is a structural issue rather than happenstance, Barça face roughly the same number of open-play shots as PSG, but concede on them 50% more often. And this is backed up by simply looking at the types of goals Barça concede.
  
Look at the goals Barcelona has conceded against PSG, Chelsea, Club Brugge, Inter, and Atletico Madrid. It's always the exact same goal: a simple ball in behind with a late man run and then a cross into the box for a tap-in.
  
Even though Barcelona don't necessarily concede more chances, the chances they do concede are basically one-on-ones with the keeper, and ultimately, that is why they've been knocked out in the Champions League.  
  
And with these glaring defensive deficiencies, you'd expect Hansi Flick would have attempted to rectify this by bolstering Barça's defence.  
  
Of the 15 signings Hansi Flick has made, only one is a defender, and the one he brought in, Joao Cancelo, is notoriously not all that good defensively. And during Flick's tenure, one could argue Barcelona’s defensive personnel has gotten worse.
  
In the last 2 years, Barcelona lost Inigo Martinez, arguably Barcelona's best defender in the 2024-25 campaign, and they've also lost Ronald Araujo to Liverpool, who, for all his shortcomings, is still one of Barcelona's best defenders.

As excellent a midfielder as Rodri is, his signing doesn't magically make Barcelona's defensive woes disappear. And I would argue that Barcelona would have been much better served by getting a world-class defender to fix their defensive frailties rather than improving an already world-class midfield.
  
Now, to address the common excuse that the reason Barça got knocked out isn't because of their defence, but rather because they missed their chances.

So let's consider whether or not Barça are actually wasteful in front of goal. 
  
Over the last 2 Champions League campaigns, Barça have scored 71 goals from a total xG of 61. That’s 10 more goals than the quality of their chances deserved, and in 2024-25 only Atlético beat their xG by more than Barça did.

And when looking at big chances, it’s the same story. Barça converted 53% of their big chances in 2024-25 and 49% in 2025-26. 

PSG, on the other hand, only converted 35% and 32%, and Bayern, 40% both years. Thus, of the 3 best attacks in Europe, Barcelona are the most clinical.  
  
And even in the ties that knocked them out, they scored 8 goals from an xG of 7.5. Against Inter, 6 from an xG of just over 4. 

Yes, they missed 5 of their 7 big chances in the 2nd leg against Atletico.

But across those 4 games, their opponents scored 10 goals from an xG of 5.4 and put away 8 of their 10 big chances.  

Barça weren’t knocked out because they wasted chances. They were knocked out because the chances they gave away were so good that the other team barely had to be clinical. 

So in conclusion, I believe that so long as Hansi Flick keeps Barcelona's offside trap, they will not win the Champions League.
