Approved by Junior on 5 September 2026. Use the current white microphone-and-script master and ICNS going forward.

## Current generation prompt

Edit image 1, the JuniorPrompter icon. Use image 2 only as inspiration for clean Apple Mac icon dimensional craftsmanship and strong simple foreground/background hierarchy; do not copy its stars, sidebar symbols or purple colour. Keep image 1's recognisable large angled broadcast microphone silhouette as the main symbol and a script page behind it. Change the blue background to a white softly rounded macOS app tile. Simplify the microphone to smooth charcoal sculpted shapes with a broad foam capsule, one clean supporting yoke, minimal tiny hardware, no photographic grain, no cables, no logos. The page behind it is pale cool grey with three broad dark grey script lines, a very restrained warm coral reading marker. Reduce glossy glass and neon highlights; gentle depth, soft natural shadow and satin materials. Bold recognisable silhouette at 32 pixels, generous spacing, uncluttered. The microphone must clearly be the dominant object; script page legible behind. Original Apple-esque icon, not flat generic microphone glyph. Single centred finished square app icon, transparent outside rounded white tile, no typography, no surrounding mockup or comparison.

## Previous generation prompt (superseded)

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished minimalist macOS app icon for JuniorPrompter, a script teleprompter. Apple-esque simplicity and excellent tiny-size legibility. Square 1024x1024. Warm off-white porcelain rounded-square tile with standard macOS icon proportions and generous clear padding. Centre one simple flat royal-blue document/page glyph, with three generous white horizontal script bars in its upper portion and a tiny white microphone glyph elegantly integrated into the lower portion of the same page. The microphone is just a rounded vertical capsule and one U-shaped support/stem, a clean symbolic pictogram, not a realistic product. Make one script line a subtly darker blue reading indicator only if it remains simple. Bold clean geometry, spacious proportions, carefully optically aligned, almost flat vector style with only the faintest natural tile-edge depth. The composition should feel as simple and purposeful as an Apple utility app, with no more than two main colours plus the ivory background. No realistic microphone, no photorealism, no reflective glass, no neon glow, no glossy chrome, no 3D extrusions, no busy stacked panels, no lens flares, no gradients inside the glyph, no lettering, no text labels, no app name, no Apple logo, no branding, no watermark, no surrounding mockup. Transparent exterior outside the rounded tile. This replaces a rejected over-detailed glossy microphone design with something restrained, minimal, approachable and distinctly about reading a script aloud.
